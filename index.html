About Me

Coming from a non-traditional background in International Business, I began my journey into Data Science and Machine Learning as a self-taught learner, driven by curiosity and a desire to solve real-world problems. Over time, my interest evolved into a clear mission: to apply technology in a way that creates tangible impact in healthcare.

My journey into tech started outside of the traditional path, but led me straight into the intersection of healthcare and data. Now as a Master’s student in Smart Medicine and Health Informatics at National Taiwan University, I’m focused on Biomedical Informatics - designing technologies that are not only intelligent, but also meaningful in real-world clinical settings.

I'm passionate about building solutions where clinical insight meets technical precision, and where ethical, explainable AI plays a critical role. I believe the future of healthcare isn’t just smart - it’s also transparent, human-centered, and inclusive.

P/S: Beyond technology, I have a deep appreciation for storytelling. To me, a portfolio is not only about showcasing skills, but also about revealing how someone thinks, approaches problems, and grows through their work.

Google DevFest Taipei 2025

Taking the stage at Google DevFest Taipei 2025 was an incredible journey into the heart of modern medicine. I dived deep into how artificial intelligence and clinical insight can work together to solve real diagnostic challenges. By exploring the power of deep learning in medical imaging, the session sparked some amazing conversations between tech pioneers and medical experts, bridging the gap between lines of code and life-saving applications.

It wasn't just a technical talk - it was a glimpse into a smarter, more human-centered future for healthcare.

Topic: "Hands-on AI for Smart Medical Imaging" Audience: 49 participants

Volunteer Photography & Human Stories

These photographs were created during volunteer activities in remote and underserved communities. Through documenting everyday moments, local environments, and personal encounters in highland areas, I sought to preserve the dignity, resilience, and humanity of the people I met.


Photography became a way to listen and bear witness rather than simply to observe. The images reflect the lives and circumstances of individuals and families who are often distant from public attention, while also capturing the warmth, strength, and community spirit that shaped those experiences. This collection is presented as a personal record of volunteer service and human connection, with respect for the people and communities represented.
